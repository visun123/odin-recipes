Uppu Seedai Recipe

Description

Uppu Seedai (Salt Seedai) stands as a significant and cherished dish during the auspicious Krishna Jayanthi (Gokulashtami) festival. Amidst the array of offerings we prepare on this special day, Seedai holds a unique and integral place as Prasad. The process involves combining rice flour and urad dal flour with a blend of salt, hing (asafoetida), and butter, which is then kneaded into a dough. Subsequently, we craft these dough into tiny balls before submerging them into hot oil for deep frying, rendering them irresistibly crunchy. Seedai emerges as a savory and crunchy snack, suitable for everyone from little kids to adults. It's worth noting that Uppu Seedai may initially pose a challenge due to their intricate preparation and time-consuming nature, but once you master the techniques, the results are undeniably rewarding!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Rice Flour or Idiyappam Flour, Fine Ground
  • 1 tsp Urad Dal
  • 1 tbsp Melted Butter
  • 1 tsp Sesame Seeds
  • 2 tbsp Grated Cocunut optional
  • 1/2 tsp Asafoetida/Hing
  • Salt as needed
  • Water as needed

Steps

  1. In a heavy bottomed pan, dry roast urad dal on low flame until golden brown.
  2. Once cooled down, grind into a smooth powder.
  3. In the same pan, add rice flour. Dry roast on low flame until it is aromatic. It should take about 3-4 mins. The flour should be free-flowing without any lumps.
  4. Sieve the urad dal flour into a mixing bowl with roasted rice flour.
  5. Add rest of the ingredients – sesame seeds, grated coconut, asafoetida, melted butter and salt.
  6. Mix them together with your fingers, you should be able to make a lump when the mixture is pressed.
  7. Add water as needed and make a smooth pliable dough. I had to use a little more than 1/2 cup water.
  8. The dough should be soft and smooth.
  9. Now pinch a small piece of dough and roll it lightly on your greased palm. There should be cracks on the surface and the ball should be lightly rolled.
  10. Repeat this with 15-20 for a batch. Meanwhile heat oil for deep frying. Once the oil is hot, set it on low flame.
  11. Add a batch of the seedai. Let it fry for 1-2 mins and then turn them around.
  12. Continue cooking on low flame until the oil stops bubbling and the seedai are golden brown.
  13. Remove from oil and drain onto a tissue paper. As one batch is cooking, roll the next batch and fry all seedai.
  14. Store in an airtight container.
